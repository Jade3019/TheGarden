Masculine body base and face-shape layers are adapted from Stardew Valley Character Creator (Masc version) by Jaz (Jazzybee) and Poltergeister.
Source: https://jazzybee.itch.io/sdv-creator-masc (`base.png` and `face shapes/face 1.png` through `face shapes/face 10.png`).
The 10 face-shape layers are composited with the base to provide 11 body shapes. Matching skin-color layers are generated from the masculine skin palettes already used by this project.
Masculine eye layers are from the same creator's `eyes/eyes 1.png` through `eyes/eyes 11.png`; they are recolored with the selected eye and skin palettes.
License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

Masculine brow layers are from the same creator's `brow/0.png` and `brow/brow 1.png` through `brow/brow 8.png`; they are stored separately so their placement matches the masculine face.
Masculine nose layers are from `nose/0.png` and `nose/nose 1.png` through `nose/nose 13.png`.
Masculine mouth layers are from `mouth/0.png` and `mouth/mouth 1.png` through `mouth/mouth 11.png`.
Masculine Mark1/Mark2 layers are from the creator's shared `facial features/` and `facial features bottom/` options.
Masculine hair and bangs are from `bottom layer hair/`, `top hair/`, and `thair bottom/`; the masculine top/bottom layers are kept separately and indexed in the creator's menu order.
Masculine bottom-hair source files: `bottom layer hair/0.png`, `bhair 1.png` through `bhair 15.png`, and `bhair 17.png` through `bhair 30.png`.
Masculine bangs source values: 0, dreadlock fringe 1–2, thair 1–24, and thair natural 1–5 from `top hair/` and `thair bottom/`.
Mark1 and Mark2 use the creator's shared facial-feature layers.
Source values: 0, freckles, elf ears, light freckles, beauty mark 1–3, eyebags, nose scar, cheek scar, eye scar, full body scars, small eye scar, and blush from `facial features/` and `facial features bottom/`.
Facial hair uses `beards/0.png`, `beards/beard 1.png` through `beards/beard 12.png`, and `beards/sideburns.png` from Stardew Valley Character Creator (Masc version).
Source: https://jazzybee.itch.io/sdv-creator-masc
Facial-hair colors are adapted with the creator's hair palettes and the selected skin palette.
Bangs are from the creator's separate two-layer top-hair set.
Source files: top hair and thair bottom options 0, dreadlock fringe 1–2, thair 1–24, and thair natural 1–5.
